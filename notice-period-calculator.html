How Notice Period Calculation Works

A notice period is the amount of time an employee is generally expected to continue working after resigning, subject to the employment agreement and employer policy.

Example

If you submit your resignation on 1 August and your notice period is 30 calendar days, the calculator adds 30 calendar days to the resignation date.

Expected Last Working Day = Resignation Date + Notice Period

Important

Actual relieving dates can differ. Employers may count the resignation date differently, exclude weekends or holidays, require a specific notice process, or approve an earlier release. Always check your employment agreement and HR policy.
