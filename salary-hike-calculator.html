How to Calculate a Salary Hike

A salary hike is generally expressed as a percentage increase over your current salary.

Salary hike formula

Hike Amount = Current Salary × (Hike % ÷ 100)
New Salary = Current Salary + Hike Amount

Example

Suppose your current annual salary is ₹6,00,000 and you receive a 20% hike.

Hike Amount = ₹6,00,000 × 20% = ₹1,20,000
New Salary = ₹6,00,000 + ₹1,20,000 = ₹7,20,000

What does a 20% hike mean?

A 20% hike means your new annual salary is 120% of your current annual salary.
