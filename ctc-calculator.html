How the CTC to In-Hand Calculator Works

Your Cost to Company (CTC) is not necessarily the amount that reaches your bank account each month.

What is CTC?

CTC represents the total annual cost an employer associates with employing you. It can include salary, employer contributions, bonuses, gratuity and other benefits.

Why is in-hand salary lower?

Some parts of CTC may not be paid as monthly cash salary. Employee deductions can also reduce your monthly take-home amount.

Estimated Annual Take-Home = CTC − Non-Monthly CTC Components − Employee Deductions
Estimated Monthly In-Hand = Estimated Annual Take-Home ÷ 12

Components that may affect your salary

  • Employee provident fund deductions
  • Professional tax where applicable
  • Income tax / TDS
  • Variable pay or annual bonus
  • Employer PF contribution included in CTC
  • Gratuity and other employer benefits
  • Other deductions specified by your employer
